Dear Procurement Team,

I am writing to express my interest in providing English-to-Kiswahili translation and machine-translation quality assurance for Raising The Village’s community training materials.

Quality assurance process

  1. Agree the intended audience, source files, terminology and work-order scope with RTV.
  2. Translate or review against the English source, adapting literal wording where it would be unclear in Tanzanian Kiswahili.
  3. Check consistency, grammar, names, numbers, completeness and layout; read the finished text for natural flow.
  4. Deliver editable Word documents, note unresolved terminology questions in a tracker where requested, and incorporate consolidated feedback.

Schedule and confidentiality

I can plan around the TOR’s four-week translation and two-week quality-assurance windows, with volume and milestones agreed in each accepted work order. RTV materials would be used only for the assignment and treated as confidential.

Community language in practice.

These are original practice examples prepared for this application. They are not previous client commissions. Edward must review the Kiswahili before they are used in a final bid.

Example 01 · Community savings records
English source

A savings group should record every contribution on the day it is received. At the end of each meeting, members should check the total together and agree on any corrections before closing the record.

Draft Kiswahili

Kikundi cha akiba kinapaswa kurekodi kila mchango siku unapopokelewa. Mwishoni mwa kila mkutano, wanachama wanapaswa kukagua jumla kwa pamoja na kukubaliana kuhusu marekebisho yoyote kabla ya kufunga kumbukumbu.

Example 02 · Farming and market decisions
English source

Before choosing a crop to sell, farmers should compare expected demand, transport costs and the prices offered by different buyers. A higher selling price does not always mean a higher profit.

Draft Kiswahili

Kabla ya kuchagua zao la kuuza, wakulima wanapaswa kulinganisha mahitaji yanayotarajiwa, gharama za usafirishaji na bei zinazotolewa na wanunuzi mbalimbali. Bei ya juu ya kuuza haimaanishi kila mara faida kubwa zaidi.
